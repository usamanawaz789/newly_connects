OUR DELIVERY PROCESS

From a real challenge
to a working solution.

Clear scope, shared decisions, and reviewable progress throughout the engagement.

01

Discover

Meet stakeholders, map current workflows, and establish the problem worth solving. Review existing systems, business constraints, and the desired outcome.

What you get

A problem statement, initial requirements, and open questions.

02

Define

Prioritize features, assess integration requirements, and choose a delivery approach. Identify dependencies and the decisions that affect cost and timing.

What you get

An agreed scope, proposed architecture, estimate, and delivery plan.

03

Design

Develop the user journeys and technical design. Review prototypes with the people who will use and operate the solution.

What you get

A reviewed experience, technical specifications, and acceptance criteria.

04

Build and validate

Implement in agreed stages, demonstrate progress, and test important workflows. Record changes and their effect on the plan.

What you get

Reviewable releases, test evidence, and documented decisions.

05

Launch

Prepare environments, migration steps, access, and handover. Validate readiness and agree the transition and rollback arrangements.

What you get

A release plan, operating documentation, and stakeholder sign-off.

06

Support and improve

Review usage, recurring issues, and the next set of priorities. Define an ongoing support scope when needed.

What you get

An improvement backlog and agreed support responsibilities.
